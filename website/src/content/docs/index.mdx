zquota asks Z.ai for your plan's quota and shows how much of each window you've used and the exact
time it resets, in your own timezone. The default view is for people. `--compact`, `--json` and
`--jsonl` are for scripts, and `--threshold` exits with code 2 when a window crosses the line you set.
